Star Eater: Privacy Policy

Developer: TheVic1 · Effective date: 29 October 2026 · Applies to Star Eater version 0.1 (alpha) on Android

Short version: Star Eater does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal data. Your progress stays on your phone.

What data the game collects

None. Star Eater has no user accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics or tracking tools, and no in-app purchases in this version. The game does not send any information over the internet.

What is stored on your device

  • Game progress and settings (gold, upgrades, unlocks, statistics, sound and control settings) are saved only in the app's private storage on your phone. Nobody else, including the developer, can see them.
  • Optional diagnostic log: off by default. If you switch it on in Settings, the game keeps a short list of game events and errors on your phone. It contains no personal information and is never sent anywhere. You can copy it yourself if you choose to share it with the developer.

Uninstalling the game, or using "Reset progress" in the game's settings, deletes this data.

Sharing with third parties

The game shares no data with anyone. When you install the game through Google Play, Google handles the download and may collect information under its own privacy policy (for example, crash reports from devices where you allowed this). See the Google Privacy Policy.

Permissions

The app framework Star Eater is built on includes Android's standard internet permission. The game itself does not use the internet.

Children

Star Eater collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version adds features that handle data (for example advertising or in-app purchases), this page will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.
